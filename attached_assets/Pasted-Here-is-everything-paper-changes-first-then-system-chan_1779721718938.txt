NEW:

E. Prototype Interface and Explanation Module

The web-based prototype implements two transparency enhancements beyond the core classifier output.

First, a per-article explanation module generates a natural language rationale for each classification decision. The extracted feature values for a submitted article are compared against the mean feature values of fake and real articles in the training set. The features whose values deviate most strongly in the direction of the predicted class are identified and surfaced as the primary drivers of the verdict. For example, an article classified as fake with a high title-body ratio and elevated exclamation density would generate: "This article was flagged as likely fake because its title is disproportionately long relative to its body — a known clickbait signal — and contains an elevated density of exclamation marks, both strongly associated with misinformation in the training data." This explanation is derived entirely from the feature extraction pipeline with no additional model.

Second, the prototype queries the Google Fact Check Tools API (Google, 2024) at classification time using keywords extracted from the article title. The API returns matching fact-checks from verified publishers — including AFP Fact Check Philippines, VERA Files, and Rappler — with verdicts, publisher names, and source URLs displayed directly in the result interface. This allows users to cross-reference the system's machine-learning verdict against independent human fact-checks without leaving the platform.
