Reigning Miss Elite Juvyel Anne Saluta of Intramuros/ARMIN P. ADINA
After another hiatus, the Miss Manila pageant returns this year, with 21 aspirants representing the city’s different barangays. And among the roster of successful applicants is an international titleholder, reigning Miss Elite Juvyel Anne Saluta.

Now represeting Intramuros in the city pageant, Saluta is a returning candidate who first stepped on the Miss Manila stage at just 18 years old in 2023. She posted a semifinal placement on her first try.

She later represented Manila in the 2023 Hiyas ng Pilipinas pageant, where she won a national title that paved the way for her participation in the 2024 Miss Elite pageant in Egypt.

Saluta became the first-ever Filipino woman to bring home the crown, and her reign has extended beyond a year already without a successor to relinquish her title to.

“The Miss Elite Organization is very, very much supportive of me joining [Miss Manila]. I asked for their permission, and they said they don’t want to get in the way of me reaching a dream of mine,” she told Inquirer Entertainment on the sidelines of the presentation of candidates at SM City Manila on Thursday afternoon, May 21.

“They also said that they’re very, very grateful, and supportive of everything that I do. So of course I asked for their permission, there is no bypassing anyone. So me joining this is a full force with everyone’s good intentions,” she continued.

Saluta was supposed to crown her successor in September last year, but the 2025 competition was postponed and the next pageant is slated for September 2026. The Philippines’ delegate will be 2024 Miss World Philippines Second Princess and erstwhile 2025 Universal Woman Philippines Sophia Bianca Santos, who has been waiting for over a year to compete abroad.

Saluta said she believes the Philippines could potentially post a back-to-back through Santos. “I’m very, very excited for her journey. I’m sure she’s gonna have a lot of fun in Egypt, and she’s gonna discover more about the girls there, and about herself even more,” she said.
